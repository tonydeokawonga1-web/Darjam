DarJam

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version. DarJam shows estimated traffic levels for Dar es Salaam. We do not have user accounts. Your location stays on your phone. Community reports are public, contain no personal details, and expire automatically. The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob.

Who we are

DarJam is operated by Tony Deogratias, based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania ("we", "us"). Contact: tonydeogratiaskawonga1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Approximate location (on-device only). If you tap the locate button, your device gives the app your coordinates so it can find the nearest corridor. That comparison happens inside your phone. The coordinates are not sent to our server or stored. You can refuse the permission and still use every other feature.
  • Community reports. When you submit a report we store the road, report type, severity, an optional note (up to 160 characters), and timestamps. Reports are shown publicly in the app and on the map. Do not include names, phone numbers, or other personal details in a note.
  • Technical data. Our server processes your IP address in memory for up to 10 minutes to enforce a rate limit on reports (12 reports per 10 minutes). IP addresses are not written into the reports database. Standard hosting logs may briefly contain IP addresses for security and abuse prevention.
  • Advertising data (Android app). The Android app uses Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. Where required, we ask for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or files. We do not create user profiles. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

  • Map tiles — the map provider receives the IP address and the map area requested.
  • Open-Meteo — our server requests weather for Dar es Salaam; no user data is sent.
  • Hosting provider — stores the report database and serves the API.
  • Google AdMob — advertising in the Android app, as described above.

Retention

Reports expire automatically after 55 minutes to 8 hours depending on type (a confirmation can extend them by up to 30 minutes). Expired reports are removed from the active store and are no longer returned by the API.

Your choices and rights

  • Deny or revoke location permission in Android settings at any time.
  • Limit ad personalisation in your device's Google settings; ads may still be shown.
  • Ask us to remove a report you submitted by emailing [YOUR EMAIL] with the road and approximate time. Because reports contain no identity, we may need details to locate it.
  • Request information about the data we hold, or object to processing, using the same address.

Children

DarJam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Reports are validated and rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information in a public report.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, note the change in the app or on this page.

DarJam is an independent project and is not affiliated with any government traffic authority. See also terms of use.
